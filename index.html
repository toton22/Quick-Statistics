About the course

DBS-PC-BR-601-T · Business Research & Statistics

MBA Semester I · 3 credits · 45 contact hours. Taught hands-on in Microsoft Excel (Analysis ToolPak, PivotTables, built-in functions) and reinforced with business cases from the prescribed texts.

Reference texts used throughout: Anderson, Sweeney, Williams, Camm & Cochran, Statistics for Business and Economics (12e); Levine, Szabat & Stephan, Statistics for Managers Using Microsoft Excel (8e).

How you're assessed

Internal assessment20%
Assignments / case studies (Excel, from the texts)20%
Project work (capstone)20%
End-semester exam (pen and paper)40%

The end-term paper is 100 marks. Learn each method in Excel, then practise writing it out by hand.
